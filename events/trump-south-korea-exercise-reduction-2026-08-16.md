# Background

The US and South Korea have conducted joint military exercises for decades as a pillar of their alliance and deterrence posture toward North Korea.

# Current Situation

On August 16, 2026, President [Donald Trump]({{ site.baseurl }}/actors/trump.html) posted on Truth Social that he had instructed Secretary of War Pete Hegseth to substantially reduce US-South Korea joint military exercises. Trump framed the exercises as "totally inappropriate and hostile" toward [North Korea]({{ site.baseurl }}/actors/countries/north-korea.html), citing his "very good relationship with Kim Jong Un." Trump additionally stated he had asked the [South Korean]({{ site.baseurl }}/actors/countries/south-korea.html) President to join US denuclearization efforts on [Iran]({{ site.baseurl }}/actors/countries/iran.html) and was told "No thanks!" The post signals a unilateral reduction of the US-South Korea security relationship without allied consultation. Phillips P. [O'Brien]({{ site.baseurl }}/actors/authors/phillips-obrien.html) assesses this as a decisive step in the collapse of the US alliance structure in the [Indo-Pacific]({{ site.baseurl }}/regions/indo-pacific.html), likely pushing [South Korea]({{ site.baseurl }}/actors/countries/south-korea.html) closer to [China]({{ site.baseurl }}/actors/countries/china.html).
