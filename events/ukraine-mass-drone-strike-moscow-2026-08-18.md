# Background

Ukraine has been escalating deep-strike drone attacks against Russian military infrastructure, logistics, refineries, and defense industrial base throughout 2026.

# Current Situation

On August 18, 2026, Moscow reported that 791 Ukrainian drones were shot down within Russia, with over 600 aimed at the Moscow area. These figures originate from Russian defense authorities and are impossible to independently verify. If accurate, this represents the largest single-day Ukrainian drone assault on Russian territory to date. The strike coincides with a broader pattern of [Ukraine]({{ site.baseurl }}/actors/countries/ukraine.html) expanding its deep-strike campaign from FPV drones toward longer-range systems, including AI-targeted and autonomous platforms.
