# Background

O'Brien frames [Laura Loomer]({{ site.baseurl }}/actors/laura-loomer.html)'s late-July 2026 trip to Ukraine not as a conversion to the Ukrainian cause but as a tactical pivot. The visit won her broadcast reach, prestige-driven coverage across pro-Ukraine media, and normalization that benefits the underlying Trumpist, racist, conspiracist project she continues to advance. Praise from pro-Ukraine voices is, on this reading, the mechanism by which her broader message reaches new audiences.

# The Visit

The trip took place around July 25, 2026. The broadcasting chain — both preparatory tweets and on-camera statements — combined gestures towards Ukraine with the full repertoire of her prior positions.

**Anti-Muslim framing on arrival.** While in Ukraine, Loomer tweeted: "One thing I really love about Ukraine is I haven't seen a single Muslim here... It's so refreshing... Russia can't say the same. Neither can the United States which has allowed unruly and violent Muslims to infiltrate our political institutions and pollute our country like an incurable disease." Hundreds of thousands of Ukrainian Muslims still live in Crimea and Kyiv — Loomer's framing was not observation but admonition. Earlier in the trip, on her way to Ukraine, she tweeted: "Ban Muslims from holding office in America."

**The Zelensky interview.** Loomer spent most of her televised exchange with [Volodymyr Zelensky]({{ site.baseurl }}/actors/zelensky.html) trying to extract from him the statement that Trump was "the best president." Zelensky deflected: "Had great results, 70-70. The best president is, first of all, the president for your country." He eventually yielded partial ground ("Donald Trump for today") and offered Lincoln as a counter-example. The interview closed with Zelensky joking that Trump "gave you a MAGA hat." O'Brien's judgment: Zelensky "will live to regret" the interview.

**The cocaine-rumor question.** During the same broadcast, Loomer asked Zelensky about a Russian propaganda claim that he and French President Macron had "snorted cocaine together" — amplifying a Russian disinformation line while ostensibly conducting a friendly visit.

**Post-Trump-speech reaction.** After Trump's primetime speech on the 2020 election, Loomer published a thread calling for arrests and advancing a wider conspiracy theory: China had "illegally acquired over 220 MILLION American voter files"; the "COVID-19 scamdemic was a Chinese bioweapon"; "China helped the Democrats steal the 2020 Presidential election"; and "It's time for people to go to jail!" Prior months had produced the arrest-Zuckerberg demand and the Microsoft-worked-with-the-Chinese claim — the thread was the latest iteration of an ongoing pattern.

**Tommy Robinson.** Pictures from the trip featured Loomer with British far-right figure Tommy Robinson, who appeared in a "DEFEND EUROPE" shirt. Her most recent tweet at time of writing was a call for Robinson to follow her example and visit Ukraine.

# Analysis

O'Brien's central thesis is that praising one "good" position from an authoritarian figure spreads their broader message to new audiences and provides normalization. The mechanism is deliberate, not accidental. Loomer's Ukraine pivot lets her broadcast herself as a Ukraine-friend, gaining the praise and reach of pro-Ukraine media while continuing to broadcast racism, election-denial, and conspiratorial content. Every mainstream voice that validates her trip helps her project reach audiences that would otherwise have rejected it.

This is the Trumpist playbook in miniature. [Donald Trump]({{ site.baseurl }}/actors/trump.html) himself alternates between pro-Ukraine rhetoric and the broader projects of the same coalition. Praising one element of their positions provides validation or normalization to the rest of their plans — and the Loomer case is the clearest demonstration that Europe's strategic instincts in the same direction (flattery, appeal to "better nature") are bound to fail.
