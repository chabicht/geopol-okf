# Background

Bloomberg Opinion columnist; co-author of "The World for Sale: Money, Power and the Traders Who Barter the Earth's Resources." Specializes in oil markets, pipeline logistics, maritime chokepoints, and energy geopolitics. His analysis combines technical infrastructure knowledge (pipeline capacities, VLCC dimensions, lightering operations) with political-economic framing.

# Key Frameworks

## Pipeline Bypass Arithmetic

His framework for analyzing chokepoint bypass capacity — comparing Strait of Hormuz throughput (~20 mbpd) against the combined capacity of the East-West pipeline to Yanbu (~5 mbpd), UAE pipeline to Fujairah (~2 mbpd), Sumed pipeline (~2.5 mbpd), and Eilat-Ashkelon (~1.2 mbpd). The gap between bypass capacity and original throughput defines the structural vulnerability.

## "Bypass the Bypass" Logic

When the bypass itself becomes threatened (Houthis targeting Red Sea/Yanbu), the analytical problem shifts to secondary bypasses (Sumed, Eilat-Ashkelon, Suez lightering), each with diminishing capacity and increasing cost.
