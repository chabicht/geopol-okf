# Background

Dr. Susan Stewart is a Senior Fellow in the SWP Research Group on Eastern Europe and Eurasia at [SWP]({{ site.baseurl }}/actors/authors/swp.html). She specializes in Ukrainian domestic politics, wartime governance, and the tension between security imperatives and democratic participation.

Her analysis focuses on the personnel-policy controversies in wartime Ukraine — including the dismissal of Deputy Prime Minister Mykhailo Fedorov — and the broader tension between wartime restraint and the maintenance of democratic norms. She examines how protest culture and civil society dynamics in Ukraine interact with governance under martial law, raising questions about the sustainability of democratic legitimacy under prolonged conflict conditions.
