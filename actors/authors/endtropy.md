# Background

Endtropy (writing as "John Doe" on the Substack publication "endtropy") is a pseudonymous analyst whose "Operation Epic Folly(Fury)" series provides a historical-analogical framework for understanding the US-Iran War. His approach draws on Napoleonic history — the Continental System, the Battle of Trafalgar, and the counter-blockade dynamics of the early 19th century — to illuminate the structural patterns of economic warfare, chokepoint control, and the cascading consequences of disrupting global trade flows.

# Key Frameworks

## Blockade Warfare as Historical Pattern

Endtropy draws direct parallels between Napoleon's Continental System (the attempt to blockade British trade) and the US-Iran counter-blockade dynamics. The historical lesson: blockades appear decisive in theory but generate adaptive responses that undermine their effectiveness. The counter-blockade — Iran's closure of Hormuz in response to the US naval blockade of Iranian ports — mirrors the British response to Napoleon: the blockaded power uses naval advantage to redirect trade and impose costs on the blockading power [1].

## Petrodollar as Geopolitical Infrastructure

The analysis of the [UAE]({{ site.baseurl }}/actors/countries/uae.html)'s swap-line request frames the petrodollar not as a financial abstraction but as geopolitical infrastructure — the plumbing that connects US military power to global energy markets. When that plumbing shows stress (swap-line requests, gold accumulation, currency diversification), it signals that the geopolitical order the plumbing supports is also under stress. The monetary dimension of the Iran war is not a side effect but a central theater [2].

Endtropy maintained from the series' first dispatch that the US would not achieve a rapid decisive victory, contradicting the "shock and awe" assumptions underlying the intervention. The war began February 28, 2026; the June 17 MoU collapsed by mid-July with renewed strikes and blockade reimposition. **Hit** — no quick victory was achieved; the conflict persisted for months and the ceasefire failed to hold.

- **April 2026 — The US counter-blockade would generate adaptive trade workarounds that undermine the blockading power** ("Operation Epic Folly(Fury) — Part 8" [1]). Drawing the Napoleonic Continental System parallel, Endtropy predicted the blockade would produce smuggling, non-dollar settlement channels, and trade redirection rather than capitulation. The bundle confirms: "The US counter-blockade accelerated the dedollarization of Chinese-Iranian trade, as the secondary sanctions on Chinese banks pushed trade into non-dollar channels." **Hit** — adaptive responses materialized exactly as the historical-analogical framework predicted.

## Open

- **April 2026 — The petrodollar system that existed before the war will not exist after the war regardless of how it ends** ("Operation Epic Folly(Fury) — Part 9" [2]). The war demonstrated that the energy flow through Hormuz can be interrupted by American policy decisions, and every available response — swap lines, yuan pricing, gold accumulation — permanently alters the dollar's position. **Observable**: post-war Gulf oil pricing arrangements; whether any Gulf state adopts non-dollar settlement; structural shift in central bank reserve composition away from dollars.

- **April 2026 — Both swap-line expansion and yuan pricing lead to a weaker dollar** ("Operation Epic Folly(Fury) — Part 9" [2]). Refusing the swap line triggers yuan pricing (demand falls); granting it expands dollar supply. Either path degrades the dollar. **Observable**: dollar index trajectory and real Treasury yield trends over the 12–24 months following the war's conclusion.

- **April 2026 — A coordinated Houthi closure of Bab al-Mandab alongside Hormuz would push oil to $120–150 and US gasoline to $5–6 per gallon** ("Operation Epic Folly(Fury) — Part 8" [1]). The dual-chokepoint scenario transforms a regional energy crisis into a global trade catastrophe. **Observable**: full Houthi closure of Bab al-Mandab transit; corresponding Brent crude and US retail gasoline price levels.

- **April 2026 — Global central bank gold accumulation will accelerate as a consequence of the war** ("Operation Epic Folly(Fury) — Part 9" [2]). Central bankers are "quietly recalculating" gold vs dollar reserves because dollars depend on a strait being open while gold depends on nothing. **Observable**: World Gold Council / IMF central bank gold purchase data for 2026–2027 showing acceleration above the pre-war record trend.

- **April 2026 — Pax Americana is slowly dying; the war accelerates multipolarity** ("Operation Epic Folly(Fury) — Part 8" [1]). The US has pushed the rest of the world toward multipolarity through blockade warfare that antagonises neutrals and allies alike. **Observable**: sustained erosion of US alliance trust; new non-Western security or financial architectures (BRICS settlement, multilateral chokepoint arrangements) gaining institutional form.
