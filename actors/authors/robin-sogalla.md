# Background

Robin Sogalla is an economist affiliated with the University of Mannheim and the Kiel Institute for the World Economy. His work centers on trade model implementation and computational economics. He is a co-author of Kiel Policy Brief No. 206, where he contributed to the implementation of the KITE bottleneck extension used to simulate the economic consequences of a full closure of the Strait of Hormuz. See [Kiel Institute](kiel-institute.html) for the institutional context.

# Key Frameworks

## KITE Model Implementation

Sogalla's contribution to KPB 206 centers on the computational implementation of the KITE bottleneck extension alongside [Julian Hinz](julian-hinz.html) and [Hendrik Mahlkow](hendrik-mahlkow.html). The model runs 100-draw Monte Carlo simulations to produce welfare and price-change estimates with 90% confidence intervals, covering 50 non-mineral product categories and their cascade through energy, chemicals, and food sectors. The implementation distinguishes short-run and long-run scenarios, with short-run elasticities set to one-quarter of long-run values to reflect limited substitution possibilities immediately after a disruption [1].
