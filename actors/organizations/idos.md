# Background

The German Institute of Development and Sustainability (IDOS), formerly the German Development Institute (DIE), is a research institute based in Bonn. It conducts applied research on global development challenges, environmental governance, and international cooperation. IDOS researchers publish through various channels, including the SWP MTA Spotlight series. Researchers include [Annabelle Houdret]({{ site.baseurl }}/actors/authors/annabelle-houdret.html) (Environmental Governance) and [Mark Furness]({{ site.baseurl }}/actors/authors/mark-furness.html) (Inter- and Transnational Cooperation). IDOS is distinct from [SWP]({{ site.baseurl }}/actors/authors/swp.html) but publishes through the SWP MTA Spotlight series.
