# Background

Laura Loomer is a Trump-aligned far-right activist and internet personality whose public persona is built on racism, conspiracy theories, election-denial, and Islamophobia. She has urged Trump to gut US intelligence agencies and extend her grievance framework into national-security policy. In July 2026 she traveled to [Ukraine]({{ site.baseurl }}/actors/countries/ukraine.html), and mainstream commentators framed the trip as a "Damascene conversion" to the Ukrainian cause. In O'Brien's reading, however, the visit was a tactical pivot, not a genuine change of heart: while in Ukraine she continued to broadcast the full repertoire of hateful, conspiratorial content that defines her broader project.

# Key Dynamics

- **Anti-Muslim hatred as a constant.** Loomer tweeted that Ukraine's lack of visible Muslims was "refreshing," comparing Muslim Americans to an "incurable disease." She separately called for banning Muslims from US office. [1]
- **Election-denial conspiracism as a constant.** She is among the most aggressive promoters of the stolen-election claim, demanding arrests of Zuckerberg and claiming Microsoft "worked with the Chinese" to steal the 2020 election. [1]
- **Trump sycophancy as operating mode.** The Zelensky interview was structured around extracting a "best president" declaration — a loyalty test she openly flagged on camera. Her broadcasting validates Trump and attacks his enemies. [1]
- **Alliance with Tommy Robinson.** Loomer has long praised the British far-right figure as a "hero." Robinson appeared in her Ukraine trip coverage in a "DEFEND EUROPE" shirt; she called for him to visit Ukraine. [1]
- **Calls to gut US intelligence.** She has urged Trump to gut US intelligence agencies and fold the 2020-theft lie into national-security policy, claiming China "illegally acquired over 220 MILLION American voter files." [1]
- **O'Brien's assessment.** The Ukraine pivot was tactical, not genuine — praising one acceptable position spreads her broader message, rehabilitates her brand, and advances the authoritarian project. See [Political Normalization]({{ site.baseurl }}/themes/political-normalization.html). [1]

# The Ukraine Visit (July 2026)

Loomer's late-July 2026 trip to Ukraine generated widespread commentary that treated her arrival as evidence of the trip's persuasive power. She was interviewed by [Zelensky]({{ site.baseurl }}/actors/zelensky.html) on television, and spent much of the interview pressing him to declare Trump "the best president." Zelensky deflected — "Had great results, 70-70. The best president is, first of all, the president for your country" — before eventually yielding ground ("Donald Trump for today") and proposing Lincoln. Loomer also used the interview to ask Zelensky about the Russian propaganda claim that he and President Macron had "snorted cocaine together."

O'Brien's verdict is that Zelensky "will live to regret" the interview. The gestures of apparent support she offered — visiting the country, echoing pro-Ukraine talking points — came bundled with the rest of her platform. The price of her support was publicizing the destructive currents in US and European politics that the trip could otherwise have ignored.
