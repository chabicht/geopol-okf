# Background

Roman Abramovich is a Russian-born billionaire businessman who has served as an unofficial intermediary between Russia and Western governments. According to sources familiar with unofficial German-Russian negotiations, Germany has used Abramovich to keep lines of communication open with Moscow. His value lies in maintaining channels and facilitating humanitarian issues (such as prisoner exchanges), not in changing [Putin]({{ site.baseurl }}/actors/putin.html)'s course on territorial or war issues. Link to [Russia]({{ site.baseurl }}/actors/countries/russia.html), [Germany]({{ site.baseurl }}/actors/countries/germany.html), [Russia-Ukraine War]({{ site.baseurl }}/conflicts/russia-ukraine-war.html).

# Current Situation

Active as a back-channel intermediary in German-Russian negotiations. Meetings held since at least early 2024 in Baku and Abu Dhabi, formally about reviving the Petersburg Dialogue (founded by Schröder and Putin in 2001, terminated by Berlin in 2021). The Russian side includes Viktor Zubkov (former PM, Gazprom board chair), Valery Fadeyev (Human Rights Council head), and Alexey Gromyko (Institute of Europe, RAS) — who carry more political weight inside Russia than Kirill Dmitriev (Putin's envoy to Trump) [1].
