# Background

Switzerland is a high-income European economy whose export base centers on pharmaceuticals, machinery, precision instruments, and chemicals — sectors where [China]({{ site.baseurl }}/actors/countries/china.html)'s export expansion has been significant. As a member of the "EU+7" bloc analyzed in KPB 213 (EU plus Norway, Switzerland, UK, US, Canada, Japan, South Korea), Switzerland is grouped among the advanced economies facing Chinese competition in third markets. See [China Shock and Deindustrialization]({{ site.baseurl }}/themes/china-shock-and-deindustrialization.html).

# Current Situation

## Third-Market Competition with China

KPB 213 identifies Switzerland as the second "winner" in the non-Chinese market segment, alongside [South Korea]({{ site.baseurl }}/actors/countries/south-korea.html). Switzerland gained competitiveness relative to other non-Chinese exporters in third markets over the 2012–2024 period, despite a modest overall weighted market-share change of −0.22 percentage points. Its excess-loss rate of 69.8% indicates that roughly 30% of its losses are mechanically attributable to China's rise — a moderate ratio, better than [Germany]({{ site.baseurl }}/actors/countries/germany.html) (80.2%) or [France]({{ site.baseurl }}/actors/countries/france.html) (78.1%) but worse than [South Korea]({{ site.baseurl }}/actors/countries/south-korea.html) (49%) or [Italy]({{ site.baseurl }}/actors/countries/italy.html) (41.7%) [1].

# Key Dynamics

- **Switzerland is a "winner" in non-Chinese third-market competition.** Its firms gained market share relative to other non-Chinese exporters despite overall losses, ranking alongside South Korea. [1]
- **Moderate excess-loss rate signals partial resilience.** At 69.8%, Switzerland's excess-loss rate is better than Germany or France but worse than South Korea or Italy. [1]
- **Pharmaceuticals and precision goods anchor the export base.** Switzerland's competitive position in high-complexity sectors distinguishes it from peers losing share in the same categories. [1]
- **The EU+7 framing places Switzerland in the advanced-economy bloc.** KPB 213 groups Switzerland with the EU, US, Japan, and others as economies facing Chinese competition in third markets. [1]
